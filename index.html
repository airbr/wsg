The WSG-o-matic.

What is this? The Web Sustainability Guidelines (WSG) are a set of international best practices developed by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) to help digital teams build more eco-friendly, energy-efficient, and socially responsible products and services. This website allows you to explore one of them at random!

Click "Open Random Guideline" to get a draft guideline in full to display. All credit to all the contributors, this work is intended to help educate.
